## Finding AI and choosing the interface

1. Open **Management → AI & Privacy**. Explicitly permit network access for a
   download, or import offline. Follow progress and use Cancel when needed.
   Refresh does not restart acquisition or enable AI.
2. Verify/test the model and explicitly enable local AI for the session.
   Restart leaves AI off.
3. Open a document from **Knowledge** or **Search**, select **Document synthesis**,
   inspect the exact Version/text and private preview, and explicitly consent.
   Results, references and receipts are in **Management → AI operations**.
   Returning to Search preserves its filters.
4. Use **Shell settings → Interface** or the guided notice to select
   **Cura · standard interface**. Select **Previous interface** to roll back.
   Instance, knowledge, language, theme and AI/network permissions are retained.

In Italian, **Apri Provelume** opens the browser application and
**Pannello locale** opens the native service panel. High-DPI actions stack and the
lower controls remain reachable through scrolling.
